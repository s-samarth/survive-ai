# Urban Survival Guide

## Escape Routes
In a chaotic urban environment (disaster or riot), escaping requires careful observation:
- **Avoid Chokepoints:** Stay away from narrow alleys, bridges, and primary tunnel systems where crowds can crush you or where you can be easily trapped.
- **Stick to the Periphery:** Move along the edges of crowds rather than the center. Keep close to walls where you have a measure of protection on one side.
- **Look Up and Down:** Don't just look at street level. Consider rooftop access or subterranean routes (subways, sewers) if the surface is completely blocked, but be aware these can become deadly traps if flooded or smoked out.
- **Identify Exits:** Whenever entering a building, immediately identify at least two physical exits that are not the main entrance.

## Building Search
If you must enter abandoned or damaged buildings for shelter or supplies:
- **Test Structural Integrity:** Do not enter buildings with heavy cracking, sagging roofs, or localized fires.
- **Slice the Pie:** When approaching doors or corners, stay wide and slowly angle your view around the corner before stepping through the threshold.
- **Limit Light Usage:** At night, use flashlights sparingly to avoid broadcasting your location. If you must use a light, hold it out to the side, not directly in front of your chest.
- **Listen First:** Pause outside the building for 3-5 minutes listening for movement, conversation, or shifting debris before entering.

## Improvised Shelter
- **Interior Rooms:** If bombing or high winds are a threat, seek rooms in the center of the building with no exterior windows.
- **Stairwells:** Concrete reinforcement often makes stairwells the strongest part of a structure, but they can act as chimneys for smoke during a fire.
- **Barricades:** Lock the door and wedge heavy furniture (desks, dressers) tightly under the door handle at an angle to prevent entry.

## Scavenging
- **Water Priority:** Check water heaters, toilet tanks (not the bowl), and melted ice from freezers for emergency drinking water if municipal lines fail.
- **Stay Unpredictable:** Never use the same route to and from your shelter twice when scavenging.
