# War Survival Guide

## Ambush Response
If you find yourself in an ambush:
1. **Take Cover Immediately:** Drop to the ground or seek the nearest hard cover (concrete, thick steel, earth). Do not freeze.
2. **Assess the Source:** Identify the direction of incoming fire by watching for muzzle flashes or dust kick-up.
3. **Move Vigorously:** If caught in the open ("the kill zone"), sprint out of the area using unpredictable zig-zag movements.
4. **Stay Low:** Crawl if necessary. Keep your profile as small as possible.
5. **Do Not Engage:** Your primary goal as a civilian is escape, not combat.

## IED Awareness
Improvised Explosive Devices are common in conflict zones.
- **Recognize Indicators:** Watch for disturbed earth, trash piles, abandoned vehicles, or wires leading from the road to the brush.
- **Maintain Distance:** If you spot a suspected IED, do not approach, touch, or attempt to move it.
- **Retrace Steps:** Carefully walk back exactly the way you came. Do not take shortcuts off the cleared path.
- **Warn Others:** Alert anyone approaching the area to stay back.

## Checkpoint Crossing
Approaching a military or militia checkpoint requires extreme caution:
- **Slow Down:** Reduce your speed well before the checkpoint. Turn on interior lights at night.
- **Keep Hands Visible:** Keep your hands empty and in plain sight, preferably on the steering wheel.
- **No Sudden Movements:** Do not reach into pockets, bags, or the glove compartment without explicit permission.
- **Be Polite and Compliant:** Answer questions directly without arguing.
- **Hide Valuables:** Keep large amounts of cash and sensitive electronics out of sight before approaching.

## Cover vs. Concealment
Understanding the difference can save your life:
- **Cover:** Stops bullets. Examples: solid brick walls, engine blocks of vehicles, large trees, earthen berms.
- **Concealment:** Hides you from view but does *not* stop bullets. Examples: bushes, tall grass, drywall, shadows.
- *Always seek cover first. Use concealment only when cover is unavailable to break the line of sight.*
