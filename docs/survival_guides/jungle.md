# Jungle Survival Guide

## Finding Water
- **Vines:** Certain thick, woody vines (like the water liana) contain drinkable sap. Cut a notch near the top, then cut it near the bottom and let the water drip into your mouth. Do not drink milky or colored sap.
- **Banana Plants:** Cut the core of a banana plant down to a stump. Hollow out a bowl in the center. The roots will push clean water up into the bowl.
- **Bamboo:** Look for older, green bamboo stalks with water sloshing inside. Drill a hole at the bottom of the section to drain the clean, filtered rainwater.
- **Streams:** Jungle streams can carry severe pathogens. Avoid stagnant water.

## Water Purification
Even flowing jungle water must be purified.
- **Boiling:** The safest method. Bring water to a rolling boil for at least 1 minute.
- **Chemical Purification:** Use iodine or chlorine tablets according to package instructions.
- **Improvised Filter:** If the water is muddy, filter it through layers of sand, charcoal, and cloth before boiling. This removes sediment but does not kill bacteria.

## Shelter Building
A jungle shelter must keep you off the damp ground and protect you from rain and insects.
- **A-Frame:** Construct an A-frame using sturdy branches or bamboo. Use large broad leaves (like banana or palm) layered from the bottom up like roof shingles to shed rain.
- **Raised Bed:** Build a sleeping platform 12-18 inches off the ground using lashed bamboo or sturdy saplings to avoid snakes, scorpions, and ground moisture.
- **Fire Barrier:** Keep a small fire burning near the entrance to produce smoke and deter insects.

## Navigation Without Tools
The thick canopy can make normal navigation difficult.
- **Follow Water:** Small streams usually lead to larger rivers, which generally lead to civilization or camps.
- **Sun Compass:** If you can see the sun, use a stick and its shadow to find East and West. The shadow tip will move from West to East over 15 minutes.
- **Do Not Travel at Night:** The jungle is incredibly dangerous in the dark. Stop moving at least two hours before sunset to build shelter.
